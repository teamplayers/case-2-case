# Commercial license

case2case is open source under [AGPL-3.0](licensing.md). That license is free for open-source use.

Some organizations need to use case2case **without** meeting AGPL copyleft requirements—for example, closed-source modifications, proprietary products, or private SaaS without source disclosure. Those uses require a **separate commercial license** from the copyright holders.

## When you need a commercial license

Contact us if you intend to:

- Integrate case2case into a **closed-source** product
- Keep your modifications or extensions **private**
- Operate a **network service** (web app, API, multi-tenant SaaS) without providing source to users as AGPL requires
- Redistribute case2case under terms other than AGPL-3.0

You do **not** need a commercial license if you use case2case in an open-source project and comply with AGPL-3.0 (including publishing source for distributed or network-hosted versions).

## How to request a license

Email **licensing@case2case.dev** (replace with your contact address before publishing) with:

1. **Organization** — legal entity name and country
2. **Use case** — what you are building and who will use it
3. **Deployment** — on-prem, private cloud, public SaaS, embedded, etc.
4. **Scope** — which parts of case2case you use and whether you modify it
5. **Timeline** — when you need to ship

We review requests individually and propose terms (fees, support, attribution, and scope) based on the use case.

## Until a commercial license is signed

Without a signed commercial agreement, only [AGPL-3.0](licensing.md) applies. Closed-source or non-compliant use is not permitted.
